I plan to keep adding to it to best optimize it and learn as much as I can then I intend to build off this foundation to make other things like transformers from scratch too. This is a part of my self study where I am going through and working on all types of ML algorithms from math. The end goal is to get a deep understanding of all the algorithms well enough to where I can better approach modern research in the field with a good understanding.
